## Cretan Rabbit Stew    

#### Ingredients   
* 1 1/2 pounds of rabbit legs     
* 1 pound of small white onions, peeled     
* 2 large cloves garlic, peeled    
* 10 whole black peppercorns    
* 5 whole allspice berries    
* 2 bay leaves    
* 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin    
* 1/4 cup dry red wine   
* 1/6 cup red-wine vinegar      
* 3/4 pounds ripe plum tomatoes, quartered    
* 1/2 and orange, cut into four wedges     
* 1/4 cup olive oil    
* 2 tablespoons Italian parsley leaves       
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

#### Preparation   
1. Season the rabbit with salt and pepper.
2. Peel the onions.
3. Peel the garlic cloves.
4. Quarter the tomatoes.
5. Wedge the orange.
6. Chop the parsley leaves. 

#### Directions   
1. Heat half the oil in a skillet, add the rabbit, and saute over high heat until browned.
2. Drain and place in a 5-to-6-quart heat-proof casserole.
3. Add the onions to the skillet, and saute over medium high heat until they start to brown.
4. Add the garlic, and continue to saute until the onions and garlic are well browned.
5. Add the skillet's contents to the casserole.
6. Scatter the peppercorns, allspice, bay leaves and cumin in the casserole.
7. Pour in the wine and the vinegar.
8. Place the tomatoes on top, then the orange wedges.
9. Pour remaining olive oil over the oranges.
10. Cover, bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer slowly about 45 minutes, until the rabbit and onions are tender.
11. Transfer to a serving platter, arranging the rabbit in the middle and surrounding it with onions.
12. Reheat the sauce in the casserole and check seasonings.
13. Add the parsley and cook for a few minutes to reduce the sauce slightly.
14. Remove the bay leaves and spoon some of the sauce over the rabbit and onions.
