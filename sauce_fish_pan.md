## Fish Pan Sauce   
[Facebook Reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1411853766896048)    

#### Ingredients   
* Butter
* Anchovies, chopped
* Garlic, minced
* Capers (with a touch of caper brine)
* Peeled lemon wedges
* Parsley, chopped
* Freshly ground pepper   

#### Preparation    
1. Chop the anchovies.
2. Mince the garlic.
3. Peel a lemon and cut little wedges.
4. Chop the parsley.

#### Directions   
1. Cook a filet of fish, skin side down, in a touch of olive oil.
2. Remove the fish when done.
3. Add some butter to the pan.
4. Add the chopped anchovies, minced garlic, capers (and brine), and lemon.
5. Cook for a minute or so.
6. Add the chopped parsley and pepper.
