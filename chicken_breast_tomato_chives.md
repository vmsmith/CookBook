## Sautéed Chicken Breasts with Tomato and Chives   

#### Ingredients   
* Chicken breasts, cubed   
* Tomato, diced   
* Mushrooms, chopped   
* Chives, sliced   
* Tabasco     
* Ketchup    
* Herbs de Provence   
* Flour
* Olive oil   
* Salt and pepper      

#### Preparation    
1. Cube the chicken breasts
2. Dice the tomato
3. Chop the mushrooms
4. Slice the chives   

#### Directions   
1. Heat the olive oil.
2. Lightly coat the chicken cubes with flour.
3. Sautée the chicken over high heat to get a crust.
4. Turn the heat down a notch or two, then add the garlic until the aroma.
5. Add mushrooms, tomato, seasoning, tabasco, light ketchup (just to coat chicken).
6. Season with salt and pepper.  
7. Continue cooking for a few more minutes.
8. Plate and add chives.
