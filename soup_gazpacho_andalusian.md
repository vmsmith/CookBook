## Andalusian Gazpacho Soup

#### Ingredients   
* 4 large ripe tomatoes, cored and cut into rough 1-inch chunks   
* 1/2 pound of cucumber, peeled, seeded, and cut into 1-inch chunks       
* 1/3 pound of red onion, peeled and cut into rough 1-inch chunks    
* 1 medium red pepper, cored, seeded, and cut into rough 1-inch chunks    
* 2 garlic cloves, peeled and smashed    
* 1 1/2 teaspoons of kosher salt (more to taste)   
* 4 ounces of white bread (baguette), torn into 1-inch pieces
* 1 cup of extra virgin olive oil (more for serving)   
* 2 tablespoons of sherry vinegar    
* 2 tablespoons of finely minced chives    
* Black pepper    

#### Preparation   
1. Core and cut up the tomatoes. 
2. Peel, seed, and cut up the cucumber.   
3. Peel and cut up the red onion.   
4. Core, seed, and cut up the red pepper.
5. Peel and smash the garlic cloves.  

#### Directions  
1. Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, onion, pepper, garlic, and salt in a large bowl and toss to coat thoroughly.
2. Let it sit at room temperature for 30 minutes.
3. Drain the juices into a large bowl and add the bread.
4. Freeze the vegetables for about 30 minutes.
5. Remove the vegetables from the freezer and allow them to thaw.
6. Transfer the vegetables and their juices to the bowl with the bread.
7. Working in batches as necessary, blend the vegetables, juice, and the bread at high speed, slowly drizzling in the olive oil and sherry vinegar.
8. Strain the soup through a fine mesh strainer.
9. Season with salt and black pepper.
10. When serving, drizzle each bowl with olive oil, a little sherry vinegar, cracked black pepper, and chives.
