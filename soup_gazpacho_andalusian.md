## Andalusian Gazpacho Soup

#### Ingredients   
* 4 large ripe tomatoes
* 1/2 pound of cucumber, peeled, seeded, and cut into 1-inch chunks   
* 1/3 pound of red onion, peeled and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
* 1 medium red pepper, cored, seeded, and cut into rough 1-inch chunks
* 2 garlic cloves, peeled and smashed
* 1 1/2 teaspoons of kosher salt (more to taste)
