## Scallops Gratin  

#### Ingredients   
* 1/2 - 2/3 pound of scallops
* 3 tablespoons of unsalted butter, soft   
* 2 garlic cloves, chopped  
* 1 shallot, minced
* 1 ounce of prosciutto, minced
* 2 tablespoons of fresh parsley, minced
* 1 tablespoon of freshly squeezed lemon juice
* 1 tablespoon of Pernod
* 1 teaspoon of kosher or sea salt
* 1/2 teaspoon of black pepper
* 3 tablespoons of olive oil
* 1/4 cup of Japanese Panko   
* White wine   

#### Preparation   
1.Take the butter out of the fridge and let it soften.
2. Cut the scallops in half if they are large.  
4. Chop the garlic cloves.
5. Mince the shallot.
6. Mince the prosciutto.
7. Mince the parsley.
8. Squeeze the lemon juice.   
9. Preheat oven to 425F. 

#### Directions   
1. Mix the soft butter, garlic, shallots, prosciutto, parsley, lemon juice, Pernod, Panko, and olive oil as if it were being done in a blender.
2. Pour a little wine into gratin dishes, and put in the scallops.
3. Cover the scallops with the gratin mix.
4. Bake for 10-12 minutes.
