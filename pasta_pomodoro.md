## Pasta Pomodoro    

#### Ingredients    
* Pasta   
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil  
* 1/3 cup onion diced very small
* 1 pound of cherry tomatoes cut in 1/2   
* 1 teaspoon garlic finely chopped
* Parmigiano cheese, grated   
* Salt and Pepper to taste
* Basil leaves

#### Preparation    
1. Dice onion
2. Cut tomatoes in 1/2
3. Chop garlic
4. Grate cheese
5. Start cooking the pasta  

#### Directions    
1. Heat the olive oil.
2. Add the onion and cook until soft.
3. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant.
4. Add the tomatoes and some basil leaves and cook for 10-15 minutes.
5. Mash the tomatoes.
6. Add more basil leaves, adjust with salt and pepper.
7. Put on pasta, add some grated cheese.
