## Foul Medames   

#### Ingredients   
* 1 cup of dried fava beans    
* 1 medium-large onion, diced       
* 400 gram can of tomatoes (240 grams of tomato)      
* Chopped garlic    
* 1 - 1 1/2 teaspoons of harissa    
* 1 teaspoon of cumin
* 2 eggs
* Tomato paste   
* Lemon juice   
* Chopped parsley
* Olive oil
* Chicken stock  
* Flat bread     

#### Preparation   
1. Soak the fava beans overnight and peel them
2. Chop the onion
3. Chop the garlic   
4. Chop the tomato    
5. Chop parsley   
 
#### Dirctions   
1. Boil the peeled fava beans in water until they are soft.
2. Sautée the onion in olive oil.
3. Add the garlic, and stir for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the cumin and let it "bloom."
5. Add 1 generous tablespoon of tomato paste and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring.   
6. Add the chopped tomatoes and harissa and cook for 8-10 minutes, until the tomatoes are "jammy."
7. Add the fava beans, and perhaps a bit of chicken stock. 
8. Season with salt and pepper, and add a few drops of lemon juice.   
9. Optional: Crack the egg(s) into it and cook them.
10. Sprinkle with chopped parsley.
