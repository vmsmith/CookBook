## Jaeger Sauce   

#### Ingredients
* 200–250 grams of fresh mushrooms, sliced     
* 1 medium shallot or small onion, finely chopped    
* 150 ml pork stock    
* 50 ml dry white wine    
* 20 g butter    
* 1 teaspoon flour, optional    
* 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard, optional    
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper    
* A little chopped parsley

#### Preparation    
1. Slice the mushrooms about 4-5 mm thick.
2. Finely chop the shallot.
3. Chop the parsley.  

#### Directions   
1. Heat half the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the mushrooms until they've released their moisture, the liquid has evaporated, and they've begun to brown. This may take 7–10 minutes. Remove them temporarily.

2. Cook the shallot. Reduce the heat to medium-low, add the remaining butter, and soften the shallot for 5–7 minutes without burning it.

3. Deglaze. Add the white wine, scraping up the browned bits. Reduce by approximately half.

4. Add the stock. Return the mushrooms, add your pork stock, and simmer gently for 5–10 minutes, until the flavors come together and the liquid reduces somewhat.

5. Adjust the consistency. If your stock contains plenty of gelatin, reduction alone may give the sauce enough body. Otherwise, you can thicken it with a little flour, preferably cooked briefly in butter before adding the liquid, or a small amount of beurre manié.

6. Finish. Taste for salt and pepper. Add the parsley and, if you like, a little Dijon mustard.
