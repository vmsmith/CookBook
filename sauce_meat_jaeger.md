## Jaeger Sauce   

#### Ingredients
* 200–250 grams of fresh mushrooms, sliced     
* 1 medium shallot or small onion, finely chopped    
* 1 cup of pork stock    
* 50 ml dry white wine    
* 20 g butter    
* Potato starch or flour, optional    
* 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard, optional    
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper    
* A little chopped parsley

#### Preparation    
1. Slice the mushrooms about 4-5 mm thick.
2. Finely chop the shallot.
3. Chop the parsley.  

#### Directions   
1. Heat half the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the mushrooms until they've released their moisture, the liquid has evaporated, and they've begun to brown. This may take 7–10 minutes.
2. Remove them temporarily.
3. Reduce the heat to medium-low, add the remaining butter, and soften the shallot for 5–7 minutes without burning it.
4. Add the white wine, scrape up the browned bits, and reduce by approximately half.
5. Return the mushrooms, add the pork stock, and simmer gently for 5–10 minutes, until the flavors come together and the liquid reduces somewhat.
6. Thicken the sauce if necessary with potato starch or something. 
7. Taste for salt and pepper. Add the parsley and, if you like, a little Dijon mustard.
