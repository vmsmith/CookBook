## Mushroom and Armagnac Sauce

#### Ingredients
* 150 g mushrooms — ideally cremini — sliced thin
* 1 small shallot, very finely chopped
* 1 tbsp butter
* 45 ml (3 tbsp) Armagnac
* 180 ml (3/4 cup) partridge stock
* 80 ml (1/3 cup) crème fraîche or heavy cream
* 1 small sprig thyme
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper
* 10–15 g cold butter, to finish 

#### Preparation   
1. Reduce some stock   
2. Slice the mushrooms
3. Finely chop the shallot
4. Chill some butter   

#### Directions
1. Cook the partridge first. Remove it from the pan and keep warm.
2. Pour off most of the fat, but don't clean the pan.
3. Add the tablespoon of butter and mushrooms.
4. Sauté over medium-high heat until they're nicely browned, about 4–5 minutes.
5. Add the shallot for the final minute.
6. Remove the pan briefly from the heat, pour in the Armagnac, return it to the heat and let it bubble vigorously for 30–60 seconds. There's no need to flambé it.
7. Add the partridge stock and thyme, scraping the bottom of the pan.
8. Simmer fairly briskly until reduced by roughly half.
9. Lower the heat and add the cream and Dijon.
10. Simmer gently for another 2–4 minutes, until the sauce lightly coats a spoon.
11. Remove the thyme.
12. Taste and season as desired (plenty of cracked black pepper).
13. Take the pan off the heat and swirl in the cold butter. 

Note: leave the mushrooms in rather than strain the sauce. And don't overdo the cream. Start with only 60 ml (¼ cup), taste it, and add the rest only if necessary. For partridge, this should remain recognizably just enriched with cream, rather than a cream sauce that happens to contain partridge stock.
