


Salt a 2.2kg whole duck all over and leave uncovered in the fridge overnight. Break it down into the legs, wing tips, and crown (two breasts together), and score the breast skin (don’t cut the meat).
Roast the legs & wing tips at 150°C for 1 hour until tender. Whilst they are cooking, make the glaze: In a pan combine 1 cinnamon stick, 2 star anise, 4cm sliced ginger, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tbsp honey, zest of 1 orange, 2 tbsp white wine vinegar, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 200ml orange juice. Bring to a boil and reduce until thick, glossy, and able to coat a spoon.
Remove the legs and increase the oven to 210°C. Add legs, wing tips and crown to a 316 Series Low Casserole pan. Brush with glaze → roast 10 mins, glaze again → roast 10 mins, glaze again → roast final 10 mins. Keep an eye on the breast and remove once it hits 50°C internal (rising to ~55°C as it rests), and rest uncovered in a warm place (e.g. a warming oven)/
Whilst resting, make the orange sauce. In a pan, melt 30g butter and soften 2 sprig rosemary and 2 sprigs thyme. Add 150ml Grand Marnier and flambée. Add 500ml chicken stock, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 150ml orange juice, and simmer for 15 minutes. Strain, and finish with butter and season with salt & pepper. Add a cornflour slurry (1 tsp cornflour + 1 tbsp water to start) - add more as needed. Sauce should be glossy and lightly thickened, not gloopy. Stir in 1–2 oranges, segmented.
