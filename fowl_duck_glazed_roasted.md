## Glazed Roast Duck   
[Facebook Reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1229491782443607)    

#### Ingredients   
* 1-kilo duck   
* 1 cinnamon stick   
* 2 star anise   
* 4 cm sliced ginger   
* 2 tbsp brown sugar   
* 3 tbsp honey   
* Zest of 1 orange   
* 2 tbsp white wine vinegar   
* 2 tbsp soy sauce   
* 350 ml orange juice   
* 30 g soft butter   
* 2 sprig rosemary   
* 2 sprigs thyme   
* 150 ml Grand Marnier   
* 500 ml chicken stock    
* 1 tsp Dijon mustard    

#### Preparation   
1. Salt the duck all over and put in fridge overnight.
2. Break down the duck: legs, wing tips, crown & score the breast.  

#### Directions
1. Roast the legs & wing tips at 150°C for 1 hour until tender.
2. Meanwhile, in a pan combine 1 cinnamon stick, 2 star anise, 4cm sliced ginger, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 2 tbsp honey, zest of 1 orange, 2 tbsp white wine vinegar, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 200ml orange juice.
3. Bring to a boil and reduce until thick, glossy, and able to coat a spoon.
4. Remove the legs and increase the oven to 210°C.
5. Add legs, wing tips and crown to a casserole pan.
6. Brush with glaze → roast 10 mins, glaze again → roast 10 mins, glaze again → roast final 10 mins.
7. Keep an eye on the breast and remove once it hits 50°C internal (rising to ~55°C as it rests), and rest uncovered in a warm place (e.g. a warming oven)/
8. Meanwhile, in a pan, melt 30 grams of soft butter and add 2 sprig rosemary and 2 sprigs thyme.
9. Add 150ml Grand Marnier and flambée.
10. Add 500ml chicken stock, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 150ml orange juice, and simmer for 15 minutes.
11. Strain, and finish with butter and season with salt & pepper.
12. Add a potato starch slurry (1 tsp cornflour.
13. The sauce should be glossy and lightly thickened, not gloopy.
14. Stir in 1–2 oranges, segmented.
15. Carve the rested duck and spoon over the orange sauce and finish with extra orange segments.
